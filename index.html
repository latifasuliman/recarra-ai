How it works

Three steps. No payment until we inspect and verify.

01

Tell us about the car

Year, make, model, zip, and how it runs. Photos help, but they are not required to start.

02

We review and send an offer

A person looks at what you sent. The first number is an offer to discuss — not a locked-in price.

Pickup and pay after we verify

We confirm identity, VIN, title, and any lien. Payment is arranged at pickup, after that check — not before.
